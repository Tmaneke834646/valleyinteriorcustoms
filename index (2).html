Questions

Something else? Call or text 231-679-0778.

How long does it take?

Most rewraps are done in a day. Starlights take longer because every star is placed by hand, so plan on 1 to 2 days depending on how many stars you pick. We'll give you an exact time on the call.

Is the price on the site the final price?

It's an all-in estimate for the build you picked. We confirm it on the call once we know your exact vehicle. Custom materials and quilted stitching are quoted on the call.

Will it sag again in the heat?

Not with the right glue. We use high-temp adhesive made for Arizona summers and back every wrap with a 2-year no-sag warranty.

How do I control the stars?

With an app on your phone. You can change the color, brightness, twinkle speed, and set them to react to your music. Most kits come with a remote too.

Can you come to me?

Yes. We come to your home or work, pull the headliner out, take it back to the shop, then come back and install it. Your vehicle stays with you. Pick "I come to you" in the builder to see the price.

Can I take the headliner out myself?

Yes. Pick "Bring just the headliner" in the builder and the price drops. Be careful around the side curtain airbags in the pillars when you pull the trim.

Can I add stars without changing my fabric?

Yes, as long as your headliner isn't sagging. Pick "Keep my current fabric" in the builder.

Dealers and fleets

We do volume headliner work for used car lots and fleets. Sagging headliners fixed fast so your inventory is ready for the lot.
